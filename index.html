Our Story

A Kerala table, set in Goa.

എന്റെ കേരളം Ente Keralam Malayalam for “my Kerala”

The name says what we want to bring to Mapusa: Kerala as it tastes at home.

Kerala cooking is built on a few things done with care. Coconut in every form, from fresh-grated to slow-cooked milk to oil. Curry leaves and mustard seeds crackling in a hot pan. Rice served on a banana leaf. Fish from the coast, spiced and wrapped and cooked until it gives. Our kitchen will cook in that tradition, with bold flavours and authentic recipes.

Goa and Kerala both live by the coast, the coconut palm and the spice route. We think the food will feel familiar here, and new at the same time.

The full story of Ente Keralam, and the people behind the kitchen, will be shared here ahead of opening.

New to Kerala food?

A few words before you order.

Kerala cooking has its own names for dishes and ingredients. Here are the ones you'll see most often on a Kerala menu.

സദ്യ
Sadya
SUD-yah
A vegetarian feast served on a banana leaf, with rice and many small dishes laid out in a set order. The centre of Onam and Kerala celebrations.
അപ്പം
Appam
AHP-pum
A bowl-shaped pancake of fermented rice batter, soft and spongy in the middle with lacy, crisp edges. Classic with stew.
പുട്ട്
Puttu
POOT-too
Ground rice layered with grated coconut and steamed in a cylinder. Usually eaten with kadala, a black chickpea curry.
പൊള്ളിച്ചത്
Pollichathu
POL-lee-cha-thu
A cooking method: fish coated in spiced masala, wrapped in banana leaf and roasted on a pan. Karimeen (pearl spot) is the famous version.
കുടംപുളി
Kudampuli
koo-DUM-poo-lee
Malabar tamarind. A smoked, dried fruit that gives Kerala fish curry its deep, sour flavour.
തോരൻ
Thoran
THOH-run
Finely chopped vegetables stir-fried with grated coconut, mustard seeds and curry leaves. Dry, light and everyday.
പായസം
Payasam
PAH-yuh-sum
A slow-cooked sweet pudding, often made with jaggery and coconut milk or with milk and rice. The last course of a sadya.
നിലവിളക്ക്
Nilavilakku
NEE-luh-vi-luck
The traditional brass oil lamp of Kerala, lit for auspicious beginnings and found in homes and temples.
The Kerala Experience

Four things you'll find at our table.

Kerala hospitality is simple: sit down, eat well, have a little more. These are the details we are building the restaurant around.

ഇല

The banana leaf

Kerala's original plate. Food is laid out on it in a set order, and you eat with your hands if you like.

തേങ്ങ

Coconut, every way

Grated into thorans, ground into curries, pressed into milk for stews and appam. It is the backbone of the cuisine.

മീൻ

From the coast

Kerala's fish cooking is sour with kudampuli, red with chilli and fragrant with curry leaves.

വിളക്ക്

Lamp-lit warmth

Brass lamps, wood and warm light. A room that feels like a Kerala home with guests over.

Ente Keralam is a Central Travancore Nasrani restaurant coming soon to Mapusa. Here is what inspires our kitchen.

കഥ
The name

What’s in a name.

എന്റെ കേരളം Ente Keralam Malayalam for “my Kerala”

The name says what we want to bring to Mapusa: Kerala as it tastes at home.

Kerala cooking is built on a few things done with care. Coconut in every form, from fresh-grated to slow-cooked milk to oil. Curry leaves and mustard seeds crackling in a hot pan. Rice served on a banana leaf. Fish from the coast, spiced and wrapped and cooked until it gives. Our kitchen will cook in that tradition, with bold flavours and authentic recipes.

Our kitchen cooks the food of Central Travancore's Nasrani community, the Saint Thomas Christians of Kerala. It's home cooking known for appam and stew, kappa with beef, duck and mutton roasts, and payasams on special days.

Goa and Kerala both live by the coast, the coconut palm and the spice route. We think the food will feel familiar here, and new at the same time.

The full story of Ente Keralam, and the people behind the kitchen, will be shared here ahead of opening.

The Kerala Experience

Four things you'll find at our table.

Kerala hospitality is simple: sit down, eat well, have a little more. These are the details we are building the restaurant around.

ഇല

The banana leaf

Kerala's original plate. Food is laid out on it in a set order, and you eat with your hands if you like.

തേങ്ങ

Coconut, every way

Grated into thorans, ground into curries, pressed into milk for stews and appam. It is the backbone of the cuisine.

മീൻ

From the coast

Kerala's fish cooking is sour with kudampuli, red with chilli and fragrant with curry leaves.

വിളക്ക്

Lamp-lit warmth

Brass lamps, wood and warm light. A room that feels like a Kerala home with guests over.

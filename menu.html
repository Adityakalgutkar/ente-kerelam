    New to Kerala food?

    A few words before you order.

    Kerala cooking has its own names for dishes and methods. Here are the ones you'll meet on our menu.

    നസ്രാണി
    Nasrani
    nus-RAH-nee
    Kerala's Saint Thomas Christians, also called Syrian Christians. Their Central Travancore kitchens are known for appam and stew, roasts and kappa.
    ചട്ടിച്ചോറ്
    Chatti Choru
    CHUT-tee CHO-ru
    Rice and curries layered together and served in a clay pot (chatti), so every spoonful has a bit of everything.
    അപ്പം
    Appam
    AHP-pum
    A bowl-shaped pancake of fermented rice batter, soft and spongy in the middle with lacy, crisp edges. Classic with stew.
    പുട്ട്
    Puttu
    POOT-too
    Ground rice layered with grated coconut and steamed in a cylinder. Eaten with kadala (black chickpea curry) or beef fry.
    കപ്പ
    Kappa
    KUP-pah
    Tapioca. Boiled and mashed to eat with fish or beef curry, or cooked with meat and masala into kappa biriyani.
    ഉലർത്തിയത്
    Ularthiyathu
    oo-LUR-thi-ya-thu
    Meat roasted slowly in a pan with coconut slices, pepper and curry leaves until dark and dry.
    കൊണ്ടാട്ടം
    Kondattam
    kon-DAHT-tum
    Food that is marinated, sun-dried and then fried until crisp.
    പ്രഥമൻ
    Pradhaman
    PRUH-thuh-mun
    The richest kind of payasam, slow-cooked with jaggery and thick coconut milk. Ada and chakka (jackfruit) are the classics.
